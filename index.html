    Accuracy and tips

    Phone GPS is typically good to about 3–5 m (10–16 ft) in open sky, and each point averages several fixes to tighten that. The A→B distance error is roughly the sum of both points' error, so expect a yard or two off at 100 yd and more under trees. Keep the phone still for the whole averaging bar. Hold it the same way at both ends. Set A, then walk toward the target reading the live distance; when it reads your target, drop your stake and set B to record it.
